# Specialist Evidence And Duplication

Three repositories represent different decisions, not three independent feeds.
Trade Bot emphasizes selected technology momentum and cash sizing. Swing's
actual primary is monthly cross-asset momentum, not a validated fast panic
system. Rotation evaluates sector leadership and transition timing. All three
derive their historical signals chiefly from prices.

At the same funded close clock and 25bp costs, descriptive full-sample return
correlations are 0.546 Trade/Swing, 0.495 Trade/Rotation, 0.664 Swing/Rotation.
SPY-beta-residual correlations are 0.265, 0.303, 0.521 respectively. Trade/Rotation
has zero direct security-weight overlap, but meaningful economic co-exposure.
No-overlap tickers do not establish independent information or incremental alpha.

Earlier combination and adaptive studies remain the performance evidence:
diversification can reduce drawdown, but tested blends/routers did not establish
superior growth versus Trade plus appropriate cash/risk controls. This cycle's
correlations are descriptive, not a new selector or future correlation forecast.

Event filings/news are genuinely different information, but the broad historical
data gate remains unmet. It belongs in context and conditional experiments, not
an admitted sleeve. No new price-only repository is warranted by this audit.

The explicit specialist-review gate checks public point-in-time data, original
timestamps, failed-security treatment, distinct information, risk-matched net
benefit, stress tests, selection adjustment and frozen prospective evidence.
Thirty independent matured observations is a minimum review floor, not proof
of profitability. Even all flags passing permits human review only: no automatic
promotion, broker action or allocation authority.

The corrective survey also bound every native capture/telemetry/event subprocess
to its own checkout source, removed credentials from inventory URL diagnostics,
and prevented ignored source/config files from evading the release fingerprint.
Trade's incomplete-period review bug now uses regularly planned exchange dates;
it does not anticipate emergency closures by reviewing on the preceding session.
The same frozen native risk slate is being rebaselined, not counted as new trials.
