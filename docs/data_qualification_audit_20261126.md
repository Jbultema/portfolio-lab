# Opportunity-Set Qualification Audit

This audit is a release gate, not a newly qualified historical dataset.

| System | Actual historical basis | Remaining limitation | Permitted use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trade Bot | Modern selected ETFs and individual technology stocks, common replay from 2006 | Survivor and research-selection bias; no dot-com-era coverage for the actual stock universe | Conditional research and monitored primary, not a reliable 22% forward forecast |
| Swing Trader | Liquid surviving ETF universe; monthly primary | Historical ETF opportunity-set selection; no proof fast experiments add value | Distinct monthly comparator, not an emergency fast-trading sleeve |
| Rotation Trader | Nine long-lived sector ETFs, current classifications; frozen strategies | Surviving funds and changing constituent definitions; accounting corrected this cycle | Rebased research and shadow, no new promotion |
| Event Trader | Public SEC/news ingestion and conditional survivor-sample probes | No broad timestamp-qualified failed-security panel with original vintages | Context and conditional research; zero admitted primary |

Event qualification already enforces seven evidence criteria, bound artifacts,
historical identity intervals known by event acceptance, actual delisted identities,
terminal treatment, at least ten years and 100 identities. This pass retains those
gates; current news, a provider sample and Boolean claims cannot substitute for
the required tables and integrity evidence. Public/free-source eligibility remains
mandatory. No paid data was authorized or bought.

Native clocks differ. Rotation is close-known/next-open/open-to-open. Trade Bot's
legacy close-return engine is not the common strictly delayed close-fill ledger.
Historical results must name the engine and execution clock. Portfolio Lab binds
each exporter to that checkout's source instead of an editable installation in a
different checkout. Missing evidence is unavailable, never a bearish market view.

Frozen Rotation rebaseline: 80 paths (five fixed strategies/controls, four costs,
two delays, two engines), each measured over full, pre-2019 and later windows.
At 10bp/standard delay the primary is 6.47% CAGR/-27.36% DD versus legacy
6.55%/-27.26%. SPY is 7.92%/-55.42%. Equal-sector true buy-and-hold is
8.09%/-52.57%, not the old free daily-rebalanced 9.19%/-52.61%.
These are inspected history, not a fresh holdout. Existing 4,400-variant archives
are preserved as legacy evidence, not silently overwritten or requalified.
