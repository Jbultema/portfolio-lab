# Research Desk Quality Standard

## Honest Benchmark

The desk is not demonstrated to equal or exceed 42 Macro. Its public research description
covers growth, inflation, monetary policy, fiscal policy, liquidity, positioning, global
cross-asset risk, and multiple horizons. The public August 13, 2026 sample also separates
near-term risks from a longer-term outlook and tracks themes over time. It is not a current
November forecast. Private subscriber tools and performance cannot be independently assessed.

Public references:
- https://42macro.com/research
- https://42macro.com/sample_reports/sample-macro-scouting-report.pdf
- https://42macro.com/education

No proprietary model is replicated or represented as our own. More charts or more signals
do not establish better forecasting, investment advice, or a reliable trading edge.

## Implemented This Pass

| Research requirement | Implementation | Remaining boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Cross-asset context | Available equities, credit funds, Treasuries, gold, commodities and dollar across 1/5/21/63 sessions | Only instruments actually present in the declared source; no stitched prices |
| Sector transitions | Absolute-return heatmap, relative leadership, defensive/cyclical basket spread, participation | Fixed surviving sector ETF universe, not point-in-time constituent breadth |
| Independent reasoning | Equity structure, participation, leadership, credit proxy and financing proxy clusters | Correlated clusters are not independent confirmations or a crash score |
| Multi-horizon interpretation | Days/weeks, weeks/months and months/quarters research theses | Qualitative scenarios, not calibrated probabilities |
| Skeptical analysis | Evidence, cross-checks, rebuttals, invalidation and native-bot implications | Deterministic templates are not an independently executed analyst/reviewer pair |
| Macro growth and inflation | Industrial growth, payroll changes, unemployment, core CPI/PCE transformations | Current revised observations; no surprise versus consensus or historic release timestamps |
| Policy and credit | Rates, real yields, curve and actual high-yield OAS | Does not infer policy intent or call HYG/IEF a credit spread |
| Liquidity | Fed assets, reserves, RRP, TGA and money-stock context | Not global liquidity; incompatible units are never added together |
| Fiscal | Deficit/debt-stock coverage and explicit units | Stock levels are not fiscal impulse |
| Positioning | Explicit unavailable coverage | No inferred COT, ETF flows, options positioning or investor surveys |
| Historical evidence | Forty-session prefix-only price and native-target reconstruction | Current-vintage macro excluded; not a blind forecast backtest |
| Bot accountability | Native held targets versus monitors, schedule mismatches and failed gates | Targets are not actual account holdings or executed trades |

## Operational Acceptance

1. Record the exact capture timestamp and latest native observation for every system.
2. Never call a successful refresh a passed execution gate.
3. Confirm latest completed NYSE session using exchange close plus fifteen minutes, including half-days.
4. Preserve old archives, current policy identity, failed jobs and incomplete outcomes.
5. Run future-perturbation tests and all native prefix checks after evidence-pack changes.
6. Inspect desktop/mobile charts, historical macro withholding, source links, and actual download receipt.
7. Separate the current macro research capture from any historical score or prospective performance claim.
